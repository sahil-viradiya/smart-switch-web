# Images

Put real photography and product renders here, then register them in
`src/config/images.ts`. Until an entry is registered, the site shows its
built-in SVG placeholder for that slot.

- Scenes (hero carousel, use-case cards/heroes): `scenes.living | bedroom | studio | hall`
- Product shots: `products[<gang variant>][<finish>]`
- Social share image: `social` (1200×630)
