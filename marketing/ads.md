# Meta ads: ready-to-use setup

For Facebook and Instagram ads. Because buying happens by message, the simplest set-up is a **click-to-WhatsApp** ad: the button opens a WhatsApp chat with your number, and you reply with a quotation.

- Button: **Send WhatsApp message** (or "Learn more" with the tracked link from `links.md`)
- WhatsApp number: +91 97255 58828
- Image ads (Feed): upload the post PNG, 1080x1350. Video ads (Reels and Stories): upload the **-ads.mp4** file, 1080x1920. Do not use the organic video for ads: its text sits where Meta puts the button.
- Text limits (recommended visible lengths): primary text 125, headline 27, description 27. The lengths below are checked by `check.py`.
- These limits and the safe zones come from third-party guides. Check the placement previews in Ads Manager before spending.
- Ads that mention a warranty or a service should link to the terms. Keep "terms confirmed in writing" in the text, as written here.
